# Haproven ki 5 Kamzoriyan

## 1. Brand Awareness ki Kami
Haproven abhi ek growing tech ecosystem hai. Bade technology brands ke comparison mein ise apni pehchaan aur users ka trust banane mein samay lag sakta hai.

## 2. Limited Projects aur Products
Haproven ke public project directory mein kuch products abhi development stage mein hain ya “Coming Soon” dikhaye gaye hain. Isse users ko filhaal limited ready-to-use services mil sakti hain.

## 3. Chhoti Team par Dependency
Chhoti team ke liye development, marketing, customer support aur operations ko ek saath manage karna challenging ho sakta hai. Isse projects ki speed aur scalability prabhavit ho sakti hai.

## 4. Revenue Model ki Clarity
Haproven ke public platforms par community aur projects par focus dikhta hai, lekin long-term revenue aur monetization model ke baare mein public information limited hai. Financial sustainability ek challenge ho sakti hai.

## 5. Community Growth aur User Retention
Haproven ko users ko lambe samay tak active rakhne ke liye regular events, useful products aur engaging community activities ki zaroorat hogi. Agar user engagement kam hota hai, to community growth prabhavit ho sakti hai.

## Conclusion
Haproven ki sabse noticeable challenges limited live products, brand awareness, aur ecosystem ko sustainable tareeke se grow karna hain. In challenges ko door karne ke liye product development, marketing, user experience aur community engagement par focus karna chahiye.

**Note:** Ye publicly available information par aadharit possible weaknesses hain, confirmed internal problems nahi.
