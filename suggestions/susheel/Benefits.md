# 🔎 Find Your Benefit

Want to know whether your college, school, coaching, or institute has a Haproven benefit?

### Step 1 — Explore Benefits

Check the available benefits and review:

- Benefit name
- Discount / offer
- Eligibility
- Validity
- Location
- Participating institute
- Application process

**[🔎 Check Available Benefits](./benefits-search.md)**

---

### Step 2 — Search Your Institute

Search for your:

- 🏫 College
- 🎓 School
- 📚 Coaching
- 🏢 Training Institute

If your institute is listed, open its benefit details.

**[🏫 Find My Institute](./benefits-search.md)**

---

### Step 3 — Check Eligibility

Before applying, check:

- Who can apply
- Required documents
- Benefit validity
- Application requirements
- Institute-specific instructions

---

### Step 4 — Apply

If you are eligible, follow the application instructions.

Depending on the benefit, you may be asked to:

- 📄 Download an application PDF
- 📝 Complete an online form
- 🪪 Verify your Haproven profile
- 🏫 Contact the participating institute

**[🚀 Apply for Benefit](./benefits-search.md)**

---

### Step 5 — Get Your Benefit

Complete the required process with Haproven or the participating institute.

Depending on the program, you may receive:

**Discount • Certificate • Reward • Internship • Opportunity**

# 🏫 Can't Find Your Institute?

Don't see your college, school, coaching, or institute?

You can request Haproven to consider adding it.

<p align="center">

**[➕ Request Your Institute](./request-institute.md)**

</p>

### What to provide

- Your Name
- Haproven ID
- Institute Name
- Institute Type
- City
- State
- Institute Website
- Why you want this institute to be added

> Haproven may review the request and contact the institute where appropriate. Submitting a request does not guarantee that the institute or benefit will be added.

Every available benefit should clearly show:

|---|---|

| 🏫 Institute | Participating institute |
| 💰 Discount | Available percentage / offer |
| 👥 Eligibility | Who can apply |

| 📅 Valid Until | End date |
| 📍 Location | Available location |
| 📄 Application | Form / PDF / Online |
| 🟢 Status | Active / Coming Soon / Expired |

---

# 📊 Benefit Status

### 🟢 Active
Currently available for eligible users.

### 🟡 Coming Soon
Program is planned but applications are not open yet.

### 🔴 Expired
The benefit is no longer available.
