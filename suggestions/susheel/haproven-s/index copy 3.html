04 Monthly Fee Arrangement

The student shall pay the applicable fee on a monthly basis. Haproven's approved support will be calculated according to the approved support rate and duration. The student is responsible for paying the remaining fee after the applicable support adjustment. The total approved support for this period is ₹360.

05 Partner Institute / Teacher

Where the concerned Institute / Teacher is an approved Haproven collaborator or partner, the Haproven-supported amount shall not be collected directly from the student. The eligible support amount may be adjusted or settled directly between Haproven and the Partner Institute / Teacher, according to their mutually agreed terms.

06 Institute / Teacher Change

  • On a change of Institute / Teacher, the approved support amount shall not be transferred directly from the previous Teacher to the new Teacher.
  • Unused support is not refundable to the student in cash.
  • Remaining eligible support may be adjusted against fees at a new eligible Institute / Teacher only after Haproven's prior approval and verification.
  • The new Institute / Teacher must coordinate with Haproven to verify eligibility and the remaining support balance.

07 Support Adjustment & Important Terms

Not Allowed Direct transfer or settlement of Haproven-supported funds between the student, previous Teacher and new Teacher is not permitted.
Allowed with Approval Remaining eligible support may be adjusted against new fees after written approval, eligibility verification and confirmation of the new admission.
Support is applicable only to the approved student, course and support period. Any unused support shall not be payable as cash. Changes to the Institute / Teacher, Course or support arrangement require prior communication, verification and approval by Haproven.

08 Confirmation

The parties acknowledge and agree that the fee arrangement, Haproven support and adjustment rules apply only to the stated support period and approved educational purpose. Any change in the Institute, Teacher, Course or support arrangement must be communicated to Haproven before continuing or adjusting the remaining support.
