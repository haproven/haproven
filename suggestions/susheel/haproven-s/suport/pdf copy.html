04 Monthly Fee Arrangement

The student shall pay the applicable fee on a monthly basis. Haproven's approved support shall be calculated according to the approved support rate, verified fee and stated duration. The student is responsible for paying the remaining fee after the applicable support adjustment. The total approved support for this period is ₹360.

The approved support rate is assigned to the eligible student and shall not be increased, reduced or otherwise changed solely because the concerned Institute / Teacher is or is not a Haproven partner. Any change to the approved rate requires Haproven's written approval.

05 Approval & Disbursement Process

  • The student submits the required application and supporting details for the fee support program.
  • Haproven reviews eligibility, verifies the fee information and communicates the approved support rate and amount.
  • This agreement is issued with the approved details and must be acknowledged by the required parties.
  • The signed agreement must be returned to Haproven for verification before disbursement.
  • After verification, Haproven may disburse the approved support amount directly to the eligible Institute / Teacher according to the approved arrangement.
  • A separate payment receipt or confirmation will be issued after the payment is actually completed.

06 Partner Institute / Teacher

Where the concerned Institute / Teacher is an approved Haproven collaborator or partner, the Haproven-supported amount shall not be collected directly from the student as a separate support payment. The eligible support amount may be adjusted or settled directly between Haproven and the Partner Institute / Teacher according to their mutually agreed terms.

The student's approved support rate remains based on Haproven's approval and eligibility review, and shall not depend solely on the partner status of the Institute / Teacher.

07 Institute / Teacher Change

  • Any proposed change of Institute / Teacher must be communicated to Haproven before the student continues under the revised arrangement.
  • Approved support shall not be transferred directly from the previous Teacher / Institute to the new Teacher / Institute by the student or either institution.
  • Unused support is not refundable to the student in cash and is not automatically payable to a new Institute / Teacher.
  • Remaining eligible support may be considered for adjustment against fees at a new eligible Institute / Teacher only after Haproven's prior written approval and verification.
  • The new Institute / Teacher must coordinate with Haproven to verify eligibility, admission details and the remaining support balance.

08 Support Conditions

Not Allowed Direct transfer, cash refund or private settlement of Haproven-supported funds between the student, previous Teacher and new Teacher / Institute is not permitted under this agreement.
Allowed with Approval Remaining eligible support may be considered for adjustment against new fees after written approval, eligibility verification and confirmation of the new admission.
Support is applicable only to the approved student, course, fee basis and support period. The approved amount is based on the information verified at the time of approval. Any change in fee, course, admission status, Institute / Teacher or support period requires prior communication, verification and written approval from Haproven. Haproven may request reasonable documents to verify continued eligibility and fee details.

09 Confirmation & Acknowledgement

The undersigned acknowledge that they have reviewed the student details, fee summary, approved support rate, support period and terms stated in this agreement. The parties agree to follow the approved fee arrangement and communicate any proposed change to Haproven before it takes effect.

This agreement records the approved support arrangement only. It does not serve as proof of payment or guarantee disbursement before the required verification and approval process is complete. Any payment made by Haproven shall be documented separately.
