Complete Procedure

How the Fee Support Process Works

Follow these steps to understand the application, teacher verification, approval, agreement and payment process.

01

Program or Event Participation

Participation

Haproven organizes a program, competition, event or educational activity. Participants take part according to that program's rules and eligibility criteria.

02

Winner Selection

Selection

The winner or eligible top-ranked participant is selected according to the program results and selection criteria. The applicable support rate and eligibility are defined by Haproven.

03

Official Support Information

Announcement

Haproven may publish the selected winner's details and applicable support information through its official communication channels. The announcement explains how the eligible student can apply.

04

Student Application Form

Student

The selected student opens the application form and enters their own personal, contact and academic information. Program details, eligibility, support rate and other fixed program settings are provided by Haproven and are not student-editable.

  • Full name and contact details
  • Student or enrollment ID, if available
  • Program or event and winner information
  • Institute, course and teacher details
  • Monthly fee information and requested documents, if applicable
05

Application Number & PDF

Student Copy

After successful submission, the system generates a unique application number and an application PDF. The student downloads the PDF and checks that the submitted information is correct.

06

Student Signature & Teacher Submission

Student

The student prints the application PDF, signs it where required and submits it to the concerned teacher or authorized institute representative for verification.

07

Teacher Verification

Teacher

The teacher opens the application using the application number or a secure verification link. They check the student's identity, institute, course, fee information and other relevant details. The teacher then records their verification and submits it to Haproven.

08

Haproven Application Review

Review

Haproven reviews the student's application and teacher verification, including winner eligibility, institute details, course fee and program conditions. The application may be approved, rejected or returned for correction or additional information.

09

Approval & Support Confirmation

Haproven

If the application is approved, Haproven confirms the support percentage, approved amount, duration, eligible fee and payment arrangement. Approval details are recorded for preparing the agreement.

10

Fee Support Agreement PDF

Agreement

After approval, Haproven prepares the official Fee Support Agreement PDF using the approved application and payment details.

  • Student and institute or teacher details
  • Unique reference number and document date
  • Approved support percentage and duration
  • Monthly fee and total support calculation
  • Payment arrangement and applicable terms
11

Agreement Signature

Student & Teacher

The agreement is shared with the student through the approved communication method. The student downloads and prints it, then submits it to the concerned teacher or authorized institute representative for review and signature.

12

Signed Agreement Returned

Final Verification

The signed agreement is returned to Haproven. Haproven checks the signatures, approved amount, institute details and required documents before proceeding with the payment arrangement.

13

Payment Processing & Verification

Payment

Haproven processes the approved support payment to the verified teacher or institute according to the agreement. The transaction details are recorded and checked before the payment is marked as completed.

14

Final Payment Receipt

Receipt Issued

After successful payment verification, Haproven generates the final payment receipt with a unique receipt number, payment date, verified amount, transaction reference and relevant support details. The receipt is shared with the student and the concerned teacher or institute.

15

Monthly Fee Adjustment & Record

Support Period

The student's remaining applicable fee is paid according to the agreed monthly schedule. Haproven records each applicable support payment or installment. Any change in fee, course, teacher or institute must be reported and reviewed before adjustment.

Fee Calculation

How Student Fee Support Is Calculated

The support amount depends on the approved percentage, eligible monthly fee and support duration.

Example of 12% Support

If the eligible monthly fee is ₹500 and the student receives 12% support for six months, Haproven's monthly support is ₹60. The student's remaining monthly fee is ₹440.

This is an illustrative calculation only, not a confirmed support offer. Each student's approved percentage, fee and duration will be stated in their official agreement.

Terms and Conditions

Important Support Rules

These rules help keep the process clear for the student, teacher and Haproven.

✓ Program Guidelines

  • Students may apply after being declared eligible under the program.
  • Support is limited to the amount and duration approved by Haproven.
  • Student information must be accurate and complete.
  • Teacher or institute verification is required where specified by the program.
  • Payment records and receipts are issued according to the verified payment status.

× Restrictions

  • Support cannot be claimed without eligibility review and approval.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee approval or payment.
  • Unused support cannot be claimed by the student as cash unless explicitly permitted in the official program terms.
  • Support cannot automatically move to a different course, teacher or institute.
  • Changes to the approved arrangement require prior review and approval.

Institute or Teacher Change

If a student changes their institute, teacher or course, they must inform Haproven before continuing the support arrangement. Any remaining eligible support will be reviewed and may be adjusted only after verification and prior approval. The student or previous teacher must not independently transfer or alter the approved support arrangement.
