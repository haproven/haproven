04. Monthly Fee Arrangement

Student ki fee monthly basis par payable hogi.

Haproven ka approved support bhi monthly fee arrangement ke according calculate hoga. Is example mein ₹500 normal monthly fee mein se ₹60 Haproven support hai aur student ₹440 monthly pay karega.

Haproven ka total approved support ₹360 hai for the complete support period.

05. Haproven Partner Institute

Agar concerned Institute / Teacher Haproven ka approved collaborator / partner hai, to Haproven-supported amount student se directly collect nahi kiya jayega.

Eligible support amount ko Haproven aur Partner Institute / Teacher ke beech agreed terms ke according directly adjust / settle kiya ja sakta hai.

06. Institute / Teacher Change
  • Student ke Institute / Teacher change karne par Haproven-supported amount directly old Teacher se new Teacher ko transfer nahi hoga.
  • Student ko unused support ka cash refund nahi milega.
  • Remaining eligible support ko Haproven ki prior approval aur verification ke baad new eligible Institute / Teacher ki fee mein adjust kiya ja sakta hai.
  • New Institute / Teacher ko Haproven ke saath directly coordination karke eligibility aur remaining support verify karna hoga.
07. Support Adjustment Rule
Not Allowed

Student, old Teacher aur new Teacher ke beech Haproven-supported amount ka direct transfer ya settlement allowed nahi hoga.

Allowed with Approval

Remaining support ko Haproven ki written approval, eligibility verification aur new admission ke basis par new fee mein adjust kiya ja sakta hai.

08. Important Terms
  • Support sirf approved student, course aur support period ke liye applicable hai.
  • Support amount monthly basis par calculate / settle kiya ja sakta hai.
  • Unused support automatically cash mein payable nahi hoga.
  • Institute / Teacher change hone par Haproven ki verification aur approval required hogi.
  • Any adjustment / transfer Haproven ke approved terms ke according hi hoga.
09. Confirmation

The parties acknowledge that the above fee arrangement, Haproven support and adjustment rules apply only for the stated support period and approved educational purpose.

Any change in Institute, Teacher, Course or support arrangement must be communicated to Haproven before continuing or adjusting the remaining support.
